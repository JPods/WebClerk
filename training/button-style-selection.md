# Training Video: Choosing Your Toolbar Button Style

**Duration:** ~2 minutes
**Audience:** All users — first day setup
**Prerequisites:** Logged into WebClerk 3

---

## Script

### INTRO (10 seconds)

**[Show: WebClerk 3 DataBrowser with toolbar visible]**

> WebClerk gives you a choice of how your toolbar buttons look.
> There are four styles — pick the one that feels right to you.
> This takes about 30 seconds.

### FINDING THE PICKER (15 seconds)

**[Show: Mouse moving to top-right of screen, near user avatar]**

> Look at the top bar, near your name. You'll see a small button
> that says "Glass" or "OSX" or "Duo" or "Min."

**[Show: Click the style picker button]**

> Click it to cycle to the next style. Each click switches to
> a different look.

### GLASS STYLE (20 seconds)

**[Show: Toolbar with Glass buttons active, picker shows "🔘 Glass"]**

> This is Glass — the classic WebClerk look. Chrome frames with
> highlights. If you used WebClerk 2, these are the buttons you know.

**[Show: Hover over Add Record — button brightens]**

> Hover over a button and it brightens. Press it and it darkens.
> Three states — normal, hover, pressed.

**[Show: Hover over several buttons, showing tooltips]**

> Every button has a tooltip. Hold your mouse over it to see
> what it does.

### OSX STYLE (15 seconds)

**[Show: Click picker → switches to "🍎 OSX"]**

> This is OSX — Apple-native style. Photorealistic icons.
> Same actions, different personality.

**[Show: Toolbar with OSX buttons, hover over a few]**

> Same hover and press behavior. Same tooltips. Just a different look.

### PHOSPHOR STYLE (15 seconds)

**[Show: Click picker → switches to "◆ Duo"]**

> This is Phosphor Duotone — modern, clean, scalable.
> These are SVG icons, so they're perfectly sharp at any size.

**[Show: Hover over a button — it lifts slightly and brightens]**

> Notice the hover animation — the button lifts and brightens.
> Press it and it shrinks slightly. It feels responsive.

### MINIMAL STYLE (10 seconds)

**[Show: Click picker → switches to "— Min"]**

> This is Minimal — just text and emoji. No images at all.
> Fastest to load, works everywhere, great for accessibility.

### YOUR CHOICE STICKS (15 seconds)

**[Show: Click picker back to preferred style]**

> Pick the one you like. Your choice is saved automatically.
> Close the browser, come back tomorrow — your buttons are still
> the style you picked.

**[Show: Close browser tab, reopen WebClerk — same style persists]**

> It's stored on your computer, not on the server. Your
> coworkers can each pick their own style.

### THE TOOLBAR ACTIONS (20 seconds)

**[Show: Slowly hover over each button in the list toolbar with tooltips showing]**

> Let me show you what each button does. From left to right:
>
> **Filter** — show or hide the filter row.
> **Show All** — clear all filters, show every record.
> **Show Subset** — show only the records you selected.
> **Omit** — hide the records you selected.
> **Sort** — change the sort order.
> **Print** — open reports and printing.
> **Delete Selection** — delete all selected records. This asks you twice to confirm.

**[Show: Hover over detail toolbar buttons]**

> Above the detail panel:
>
> **Add** — create a new blank record.
> **Save** — save your changes.
> **Discard** — throw away unsaved changes.
> **Delete** — delete this one record.
> **Print** — print or report on this record.

### WRAP UP (10 seconds)

**[Show: Full DataBrowser with preferred style active]**

> That's it. Pick your buttons, learn the eleven actions,
> and you're ready to work. Every toolbar in WebClerk
> uses these same buttons in the same order.

---

## Recording Notes

- Total time: approximately 2 minutes
- Record at 1920×1080 on dark theme for best icon visibility
- Use the Contact model (or Action model) — has enough records to demo selection
- For the "choice sticks" section, actually close and reopen the browser to prove persistence
- Speak at a relaxed pace — these are new users, don't rush
- Background music: none (distracting for training)
