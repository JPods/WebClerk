Actions are the spine. Staff creates actions as they work. Each action's
  refs.links points to whatever they touched (order, invoice, purchase,
  customer). The dashboard reads those links and aggregates the dollar values
  into summary cards. Click a card → see the transactions. Click a transaction →
   opens in new window.

  No new API endpoints — it's just wcapi/get/action filtered by staff + date,
  then client-side aggregation from the refs.links arrays.

  The denormalized summary in refs.links is a cache written at link-time —
  acceptable staleness, truth lives in the transaction record.
