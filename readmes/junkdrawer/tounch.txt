
⏺ Done. Here's how it works now:                       
                                                                      
  "+ Add Touch..." is a select dropdown with the 5 channel types (📞 Call, ✉️    
  Email, 🏢 Visit, 💬 Text, 🤝 Meeting). When you pick one:                     
                                                                                
  - An inline form expands right there in the touch panel                       
  - Direction selector (Outbound/Inbound)                                       
  - Subject field (auto-focused)                                                
  - Summary textarea                                              
  - Save/Cancel buttons                                                         
  - Save creates the touch via saveRecord('touch', ...) linked to the action,
  reloads the touch list, shows a toast                                         
                                                                  
  No new browser page. Everything stays in the card.
