  Good guidance. Key decisions noted:

  Usage — sales accumulation records, not "how the product is used." Track what sold,
  how much, when. This feeds margin velocity and Alice's pattern recognition.

  Variants — You're right to question it. The tradeoff:
  - Individual items: More records, but each has its own inventory count, cost, price,
  GL, vendor, manufacturer. No ambiguity. Every SKU is a row. Inventory control is
  precise.
  - Variants: Matches how people browse ("show me this shirt") but inventory becomes a
  nested problem. "Do we have size M in blue?" requires a second lookup inside the
  variant.

  Individual items with a parent_item_id (or variant_group) link gives you both — each
  size/color is its own item with full inventory control, but they're grouped for
  display. The Item model already has a variants JSONField and variant_attrs. That's
  the right structure — group for browsing, individual for operations.

  Warehouse and Catalog — I'll check wc2 for the patterns. If they're simple table
  structures, worth seeding now. If they're complex workflow systems, defer.
